## curl spot-check one-liners
```bash
for p in .env .git/config wp-config.php.bak backup.zip db.sql; do
  code=$(curl -s -o /dev/null -w "%{http_code}" https://target.com/$p)
  echo "$p -> $code"
done
```
Any `200` and you have a finding — verify content, screenshots, then write it up.
