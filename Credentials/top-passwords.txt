## Usage
- Password spraying > brute force (no lockout risk)
- Loop: for each of the top 20, try against ALL usernames; stop on lockout
- Hydra: `hydra -l admin -P top-passwords.txt target http-post-form ...`
- WPScan: `--passwords top-passwords.txt --usernames <users.txt> --force`
